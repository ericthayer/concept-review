## Original concept illustration

`assets/illustrated-stegosaurus.webp` is original AI-generated decorative concept
art, made with the built-in Imagegen tool. It is labelled in the interface and
Sources & artwork dialog. It is not a museum specimen or scientific reconstruction.
The orbit and fossil-outline SVGs are original decorative illustrations.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: decorative hero cutout for an independent museum redesign concept called Wonder, Panel by Panel. Create one sophisticated editorial comic illustration of a curious friendly Stegosaurus in three-quarter side view, looking upward with a quietly amused expression, tail swooping to the side. Full animal visible with generous margin, no cropping. Ink-and-paper illustration with bold near-black hand-inked contours, sage and lime green body, warm cream belly, muted coral plates, sparse fine halftone shading, elegant natural-history-meets-independent-science-magazine aesthetic. Slightly whimsical, not babyish, not 3D, not photographic, no giant eyes. Isolated on a genuinely transparent background. No letters, text, labels, logo, scenery, panels or borders. This is clearly an original illustrative character, not a documentary representation of a museum specimen. Composition fits a broad horizontal panel and retains the complete silhouette.
