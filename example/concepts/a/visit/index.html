Good questions.

Can we borrow a stroller or wheelchair?

Strollers and non-motorized wheelchairs are available first come, first served at the information desk. They cannot be reserved ahead. Motorized carts are not available to rent.

Do members need admission reservations?

Members can bring their member card for general admission. A separate timed ticket is required for the theater, planetarium, and some temporary exhibitions.

Are there lockers?

Lockers are available at no charge. See the official visit page for the latest visitor information.

What should we know about closures?

Prehistoric Journey is closed October 27–29 for deep cleaning. The Gems & Minerals Hall is temporarily closed for renovation. Confirm current notices on dmns.org.
